## The differences in m490 modules

_WBS TECHNICAL SUPPORT · Sep 3, 2012|45_

> Oh well, No replies to my question. Anyway, incase someone else comes across this thread and wants to know the answer This is what I have found out so far:
> The 490A and 490B are mic modules. The M490C, and 490D are line modules. They have input transformers.
> The M490E, F, G,H are transformerless line modules. The F is a stereo line module.
